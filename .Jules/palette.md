## 2024-06-11 - Fixed Nested Clickable Elements & Accessible Decorative Images
**Learning:** Adding interactive elements like "Buy now" or a "Wishlist" button within a parent container that is also clickable (for navigation) leads to unintended events firing due to event bubbling, breaking expected behavior. Also, having visually decorative elements like rating stars announced repetitively by screen readers decreases the usability for visually impaired users.
**Action:** Always ensure nested interactive elements use `e.preventDefault()` and `e.stopPropagation()` in their `onClick` handlers. Make sure strictly decorative components (like star ratings in UI) use `aria-hidden="true"` on their container and empty `alt=""` text on specific items to streamline screen reader announcements.
