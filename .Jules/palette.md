## 2024-07-23 - Cart Quantity Control Accessibility
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons used for cart quantity adjustments (`decrease_arrow`, `increase_arrow`) often lack accessible names. Furthermore, the nested `Image` elements frequently have redundant `alt` text (e.g., `alt="decrease_arrow"`), causing screen readers to announce the decorative image path/name rather than the button's action, while lacking visual focus indicators for keyboard users.
**Action:** Always add explicit `aria-label`s (e.g., "Decrease quantity", "Quantity", "Increase quantity") to adjustment buttons and inputs. Apply `focus-visible:ring-2 outline-none` for keyboard focus states, and ensure nested decorative image elements have `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` to prevent redundant screen reader noise.
