## 2024-07-02 - Accessible Visual Ratings & Nested Buttons
**Learning:** Visual ratings constructed with multiple individual SVG or image stars inside an interactive container can lead to overwhelming and redundant screen reader announcements (e.g., announcing "star" 5 separate times). Also, nesting interactive elements (like a wishlist button inside a clickable product card) requires careful event handling (like `e.stopPropagation()`) and keyboard focus styling (like `focus-visible:ring-2`) to ensure both elements remain accessible and do not inadvertently trigger parent click events.
**Action:** Always group visual rating elements inside a container with `aria-hidden="true"` and pair them with a text-based alternative (like a paragraph displaying "4.5 out of 5"). For nested buttons, ensure explicit `onClick={(e) => e.stopPropagation()}` and proper focus indicators independent of the parent container's styles.
